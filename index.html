The KCL/UCL Junior Geometry Seminar is a joint seminar of King's College London and University College London, presenting topics from Algebraic Geometry, Differential Geometry, Geometric Analysis, Geometric Group Theory, Topology, and related areas.

The target audience is young researchers — in particular PhD students — with the goal of being useful to those that attend. Talks can be introductory, or more advanced. All sessions of this seminar are held on Mondays at King's College in room (TBD) on Mondays from 18:30 to 19:30 , and pizzas will be provided. If you would like to see a particular topic, or give a talk, please email one of the organizers:

  • Simon Alonso (s.alonso24@imperial.ac.uk)
  • Zhaoyang Liu (Zhaoyang.1.liu.24@kcl.ac.uk)
  • Fulin Zhong (fulin.zhong.24@ucl.ac.uk)

The format of this seminar is slightly different from the usual weekly talk format. This year we will have one expert speaker every three weeks (occasionally two) and hold two preparatory sessions (or occasionally only one) on the subject of the expert talk. These sessions will be cooperative and revolve around discussions and questions posed by the expert. The preliminary sessions will sometimes begin with a brief exposition of the questions by the chair, but their main goal is to encourage discussion and exchange. Whether you already know a lot about the subject or are simply curious to learn more, you are very welcome to attend: the diversity of perspectives is especially valuable when learning mathematics!

This seminar is run in parallel with the Imperial Junior Geometry Seminar , which features one talk every week, and we strongly encourage everyone to attend both.

Past Talks

  • Title: Advanced Potion-Making — Secrets from the Half-Blood Prince

    Speaker: Professor Horace Slughorn (Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry)

    Tuesday 21st October, 4:00pm–5:00pm, Dungeon Classroom 3, Hogwarts Castle

    In this lecture we will brew the Draught of Living Death, a potion of unparalleled power and subtlety.

    We will begin by introducing the magical properties of key ingredients such as asphodel, wormwood, valerian root, and sopophorous bean. The seminar will then present advanced brewing techniques including precise stirring methods and timing charms, crucial for avoiding catastrophic side effects. Finally, we will discuss the wider applications of potion-making in magical society and the ethical responsibilities of advanced practitioners.
